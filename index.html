Wilkinsonart Photography makes pictorial art photographs in and around Westerly, Rhode Island. Most of the work is landscape and coastal scenes, printed to order and mounted in simple frames. Prints can be ordered by email, and portrait and event bookings are taken when the calendar allows. Please email with the size and subject you have in mind and we will reply with details.

Questions? Write to hello@wilkinsonartphotography.com.
